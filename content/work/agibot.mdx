## Context

AGIBOT builds humanoid robots. I spent the summer between my two MBA years with its team in Los Angeles, working on product and go-to-market for the US.

## What I did

- Benchmarked AGIBOT's humanoids against Unitree's across locomotion, manipulation, autonomy and safety.
- Turned field diagnostics and enterprise customer feedback into product requirement documents, then prioritized them into a hardware-software roadmap.
- Built the US go-to-market plan across 5+ target logistics and manufacturing sites and 10+ startup and research-lab partners, chosen for high-ROI physical AI use cases.
