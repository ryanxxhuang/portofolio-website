## The problem

General contractors juggle documentation, compliance tracking and coordination across many parties on every site. CMIS.ai automates that work. I started it on what I learned in five years of delivering public-works programs.

## What I built

- The PRDs and a multi-agent architecture.
- A vision pipeline that turns site photos into structured construction records.

## Where it stands

Two pilot users are lined up: an engineering consultancy and a general contractor in Taiwan. I'm driving on-site adoption with both.
