## The lab

Prof. Yuchen Cui's lab at UCLA builds robots that assist people in everyday environments. Its work covers better robot training data, more dependable foundation models, and autonomy designed to work alongside people.

## The two questions I proposed

### Do learned policies keep the safety margins people demonstrate?

When people demonstrate a manipulation task, they usually keep some distance from collisions. The question is whether a policy trained on those demonstrations inherits that margin, shrinks it or loses it. That matters for any learned robot working near people or fragile objects, and it says something about what good training data looks like.

### How much do physics simulators disagree, and does it matter?

Different physics solvers can simulate the same contact-rich scene differently. The question is how large that disagreement is for manipulation tasks, and how it affects policies that are trained in simulation and then moved to real robots.

## Status

Both questions are at the proposal stage.
