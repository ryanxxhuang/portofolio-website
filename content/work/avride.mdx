## The partner

Avride runs more than 1,000 delivery robots on over 25 U.S. college campuses. I brought Avride in as our capstone partner, and I lead a five-person MBA team on its next growth phase.

## The questions we're answering

- **Pricing.** A volume-tiered model that makes smaller campuses profitable to serve.
- **Market selection.** A go/no-go framework for new campus and city markets that weighs order volume, climate, geography and dining-partner fit.
- **Expansion.** The U.S. first, followed by an international market-entry assessment.

## Status

In progress through spring 2027.
