Midway through the Pure Pursuit lab, the steering servo on the real car stopped responding. The cause was one level down the stack: VESC firmware 6.06 for the 60_MK6 controller shipped with a broken servo output path, and the prebuilt binaries only covered the MKIII, MKV, PLUS and Flipsky boards.

## What I did

1. Kept the software moving in the AutoDRIVE simulator so the lab wasn't blocked.
2. Cross-compiled VESC from the `release_6_06` branch for the MK6 inside a Docker container.
3. Flashed the build. The servo came back.

## What I took from it

Robotics is half writing controllers and half knowing when to drop a level of the stack.
